# Music bed

Drop copyright-safe music tracks here (.mp3/.m4a/.wav/.ogg) — the renderer
picks one at random per reel and mixes it quietly under the narration.

ONLY use tracks you're licensed to publish:
  - Pixabay Music   (https://pixabay.com/music/  — free commercial use)
  - YouTube Audio Library (via YouTube Studio — free, check per-track terms)
  - Anything you bought/produced

Do NOT put trending platform sounds here — those are licensed only inside
the platforms' own editors, not for burned-in audio.

Empty folder = reels render without music (use --no-music to force off).
